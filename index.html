About us

Rooted in Basavanagudi, built for business.

Srinivasa Marktech Private Limited is a Bengaluru-based company operating from its principal place of business on Dr. D.V.G. Road in Basavanagudi, one of the city's oldest and most established neighbourhoods.

The company is incorporated as a private limited company, led by its two directors, and holds a regular registration under the Goods and Services Tax regime in the State of Karnataka.
